## Fixes
1. **Per-item validation** (`models/domain_validation.py`): each Pydantic error is traced to its deepest list item, and
   that item is dropped. An error outside a list resets the field to its default. The rest of the block is kept, and
   a block that still fails becomes the model defaults. Nothing is passed through raw. Counts go to
   `pipeline.extraction._dropped` and stay off the SSE stream.
2. **Plan-time reconcile** (`pipeline/plan_reconcile.py`), run after the plan returns:
   - Triggers: the dataSource's `requiresEvidence` key is false in the plan's evidence, or its domain is not in
     contentTags/modifiers.
   - Action: re-point the tab to a same-component dataSource in an active domain (declared siblings first), else drop it.
   - Record: `pipeline.reconcile`; drops also go to `droppedTabs`.
   - The toolkit now marks evidence-gated paths (`tech.snippets [has_code]`) and `plan.txt` states your rule.
3. **Plan output budget** (`plan.txt`):
   - Caps: `brief.what` ≤ 25 words, terms ≤ 12 × 48 chars, `goal` ≤ 15 words, prose ≤ 20 words.
   - Format: compact single-line JSON.
   - The examples were trimmed to the same caps.
