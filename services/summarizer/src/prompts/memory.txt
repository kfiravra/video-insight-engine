<outline_rules>
Split the video into 4–12 sections at its real topic shifts. A video shorter than 4 minutes gets at most one section per full minute.
- "start" and "end" use the transcript's timestamp format (m:ss, or h:mm:ss past one hour). Anchor every boundary on the [m:ss] marker nearest to where the topic changes.
- The first section starts at 0:00. The last section ends at {duration}.
- Sections are contiguous and never overlap: each section ends exactly where the next one starts.
- Every section is at least 1 minute long.
- Spread the sections across the WHOLE video — never cluster them in the first part.
- "title": at most 8 words naming what happens in that section, with its specifics ("toast chiles, blend, braise" — not "Cooking").
</outline_rules>

<evidence_rules>
Answer EVERY key below. true only when the transcript itself contains that content — said or shown, not implied by the topic or the title. When unsure, false.
- has_steps: ordered steps the viewer can follow (a recipe method, build steps, a setup procedure)
- has_ingredients: food ingredients named in the video, usually with amounts
- has_materials: materials, tools or parts listed for a build or project
- has_code: actual code, commands or configuration shown or dictated
- has_drills: repeatable practice — exercises with sets or reps, pronunciation or skill drills
- has_comparison: two or more products, options or methods evaluated against each other
- has_ranking: an explicit ranking, top-N list or tier list
- has_lineup: team lineups or formations
- has_claims: factual claims about real-world events that can be checked
- has_lyrics: song lyrics sung or quoted at length
- has_itinerary: places visited or recommended in an order — a route or a day plan
- has_packing: a packing or gear list for a trip
- is_learnable: concepts or facts worth testing yourself on afterwards
</evidence_rules>

<summary_rules>
- "tldr": one sentence of at most 150 characters with the video's angle — what makes THIS video different — not its topic.
- "takeaways": 3–5 items. Each one carries a specific number, name or measurement from the video. No generic advice.
</summary_rules>

<output_language>
Write the outline titles, the tldr and the takeaways in English, even when the transcript is in another language — translate the meaning, do not copy the original language. Numbers and units stay exactly as said.
</output_language>
