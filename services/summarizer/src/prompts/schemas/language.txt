RULES:
- Preserve exact target language text — no paraphrasing
- Include pronunciation guides whenever provided
- Drills must be actionable — clear prompt and expected answer
- Vocabulary items need part of speech for language learners
- Group phrases by topic/situation when possible
- Include timestamps for key pronunciation demonstrations
