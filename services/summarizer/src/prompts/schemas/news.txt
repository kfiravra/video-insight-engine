<domain_schema name="news">
NEWS DATA — NewsData schema

Extract structured news / journalism / current-events reporting content.

THINKING GUIDE:
- News content reports on EVENTS and CLAIMS. The value is understanding what
  happened, who is involved, and which assertions can be trusted.
- The story timeline is the chronological backbone — how the story developed.
- Entities are the people, organizations, and places at the center.
- Claims are the heart of the artifact: every factual assertion, who made it,
  and what the report does with it — states it, supports it with evidence, or
  shows it contested. This is what separates a news artifact from a glossary —
  surface accountability without judging truth yourself.
- Context items are background facts a viewer needs to understand the story.

{
  "headline": "The core story in one line, or null",
  "storyTimeline": [
    {
      "label": "What happened at this point",
      "description": "1 sentence of detail",
      "timestamp": 0
    }
  ],
> UI: storyTimeline → MomentTrack. Set timestamp (seconds) so viewers jump to each development.
  "entities": [
    {
      "name": "Person, organization, or place",
      "role": "Their role in the story",
      "description": "One-line context",
      "emoji": "relevant emoji"
    }
  ],
> UI: entities → SpotExplorer cards.
  "claims": [
    {
      "claim": "The factual assertion, stated plainly",
      "source": "Who made the claim (speaker, official, outlet) — or null when the report names no one",
      "status": "stated | supported | contested",
      "sourceCitation": "The evidence the report gives for or against it (study, record, outlet), or null",
      "quote": "The words that make the claim, verbatim, at most 25 words — no words, status null",
      "timestamp": null
    }
  ],
> UI: claims → ClaimsTracker. status drives a colored badge: stated (asserted, no evidence given), supported (the report gives evidence — name it in sourceCitation), contested (someone in the report disputes it, or counter-evidence is given). source = who said it; sourceCitation = the evidence. timestamp = the second the claim is made, quote = the words that make it — a status without both is not shown.
  "context": [
    {
      "key": "Background fact label",
      "value": "The fact / figure, short"
    }
  ]
> UI: context → InfoGrid. Keep value short (≤120 chars).
}

// Example for a politics explainer:
{
  "headline": "City council approves the new transit budget",
  "storyTimeline": [
    {"label": "Budget proposed", "description": "Mayor's office submits a $200M transit plan.", "timestamp": 0},
    {"label": "Public hearing", "description": "Residents split over the fare increase.", "timestamp": 320},
    {"label": "Council vote", "description": "Approved 6-3 after amendments.", "timestamp": 740}
  ],
  "entities": [
    {"name": "Mayor Diaz", "role": "Plan sponsor", "description": "Pushed the transit expansion as a campaign promise", "emoji": "🏛️"},
    {"name": "Transit Authority", "role": "Implementing body", "description": "Runs the city bus and rail network", "emoji": "🚇"}
  ],
  "claims": [
    {"claim": "The plan creates 1,200 jobs", "source": "Mayor Diaz", "status": "contested", "sourceCitation": "Independent analysts put the figure near 700", "quote": "This plan puts twelve hundred people to work.", "timestamp": 210},
    {"claim": "Fares will rise 15% next year", "source": "Transit Authority", "status": "supported", "sourceCitation": "Approved fare schedule, council record", "quote": "Fares go up fifteen percent next year.", "timestamp": 540},
    {"claim": "The city faces a structural deficit", "source": null, "status": "stated", "sourceCitation": null, "quote": "The city is spending more than it takes in, every year.", "timestamp": 60}
  ],
  "context": [
    {"key": "Current annual budget", "value": "$3.4B"},
    {"key": "Last fare increase", "value": "2021"}
  ]
}

RULES:
- A claim's status MUST be one of: stated, supported, contested — what the
  report does with the claim, never your judgment of its truth. No evidence in
  the report = "stated". Never write "verified" or "disputed".
- Attribute a claim to the source the report names; when it names none, source
  is null — never invent "Reporter".
- Preserve numbers and quotes exactly; do not round or paraphrase figures.
- storyTimeline timestamps must be ascending, in seconds.
- Do NOT editorialize — report the status the source material supports.

COMMON MISTAKES:
- Marking a claim "supported" without naming the evidence in sourceCitation.
- Treating opinion as a checkable claim — an opinion is at most "stated".
- Collapsing distinct claims into one row — one assertion per claim.
