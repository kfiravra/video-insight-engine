RULES:
- Include all exercises with sets, reps, and rest periods
- Exercise names must be exact: "Bulgarian Split Squats" not "single-leg squats"
- Form cues preserved: "keep elbows at 45 degrees" not "keep arms bent"
- Include all modifications mentioned (beginner/advanced alternatives)
- Tempo when specified: "3 seconds down, 1 second up"
- Weight recommendations when given: "start with 10-15 lbs"
- Warmup and cooldown must be included if the instructor covers them
